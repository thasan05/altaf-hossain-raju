## Users

- **Secondary:** Keynote event organizers, university business school leadership, and symposium chairs seeking speaker talent and masterclass trainers on personal branding, corporate readiness, and sales execution.

## Product Purpose

Serve as the definitive executive digital portfolio and professional authority platform for **Md. Altaf Hossain Raju, PMP®**. It provides verifiable proof of cross-industry operational leadership, quantifiable commercial achievement across global multinationals (Marico Bangladesh, Robi Axiata, Nestlé Bangladesh), and sustained social impact as founder of Bangladesh's largest professional youth community (LCB). Success means converting visitor engagement into executive recruiter dialogues, corporate partnerships, and high-profile keynote/masterclass engagements.

## Positioning

**Boardroom Rigour Meets Grassroots Impact:** The distinct synthesis of a battle-tested multinational commercial leader (Area Manager at Marico Bangladesh, PMP® certified, two-time Global CEO Award winner, IBA DU Executive MBA) who also architected Bangladesh's premier 24,000+ member professional youth network. Unlike conventional executives disconnected from talent pipelines or campus mentors lacking multi-million dollar P&L operational discipline, Raju operates at the nexus of commercial distribution excellence and youth capability transformation.

## Operating Context

## Capabilities and Constraints

- High-performance, fully responsive single-page web experience built with Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, and Framer Motion.
- Comprehensive semantic structured data (Schema.org JSON-LD for Person, ProfilePage, and Organization) to optimize search indexing and professional knowledge graph presence.
- Modular narrative sections: Verified Impact Metrics, Executive Biography, Interactive Career Timeline (Marico, Robi, Nestlé), Strategic Ventures (LCB & Skill Hunt), Core Competencies & Expertise Matrix, Speaking Engagements & Masterclasses, Academic & Professional Credentials, Media Coverage, Verified Testimonials, and Contact Inquiries.
- **Durable Constraint:** Absolute truth in reporting. Every metric (24,000+ members, 192+ sessions, 2,600+ students, 7+ years MNC experience), corporate title, and award must strictly match official documentation. No fabricated testimonials or unverified metrics.

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence Over Assertion:** Every claim of operational success or social impact must be accompanied by concrete numbers, verified awards, or authentic photographic documentation.
2. **Dual-Helix Authority:** Commercial corporate execution and grassroots youth capability development are mutually reinforcing pillars; neither is obscured.
3. **Executive Velocity:** Present critical credentials, metrics, and role history within the first five seconds of scanning on any screen size.
4. **Authenticity Without Fabrication:** Retain genuine quotes, verifiable contact paths, and exact historical institutional relationships.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA standards for contrast ratios across all typography and interactive surfaces.
- Fully accessible keyboard navigation, semantic HTML landmarks (`header`, `main`, `section`, `footer`), clear skip navigation targets, and descriptive alternative text for all imagery.
- Respect for user system preferences including `prefers-reduced-motion` across animated timelines and cards.
