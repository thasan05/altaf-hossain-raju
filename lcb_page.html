The story

Where ambition, finds its people.

Est. 2017 · Dhaka, Bangladesh

LCB started with a simple belief — Bangladesh's next generation deserved a professional community built around growth, opportunity, and connection. Today, it has become the country's first LinkedIn-based professional movement. Students, founders, creators, and future leaders learning in public, building together, and opening doors for one another. This is more than networking. It's momentum.

Two years on, it's the country's first LinkedIn-based community. Campus Leads in every major university. Masterclasses with founders and recruiters. A culture of building in public, showing your work, and lifting people up as you climb.

We don't do meetups. We move together.

EventQuick view

Oct 16, 2026

Featured

LinkedIn Community Bangladesh (LCB) has organised its Get-together 3.0, bringing together the vision of youth empowerment and professional networking through an initiative designed to connect young people with corporate professionals.

The initiative focuses on expanding professional networks, encouraging meaningful interactions, and opening new pathways for career development. 


By creating opportunities for young people and experienced professionals to connect, LCB seeks to help bridge the gap between academic life and the professional world.

In an increasingly competitive job market, professional relationships, knowledge-sharing, and access to career insights can play a vital role in shaping future opportunities. 


Through initiatives such as Get-together 3.0, LCB continues to emphasise the importance of building a supportive professional community where individuals can connect, exchange experiences, and grow together.

Beyond networking, the gathering reflects the value of community-driven initiatives in empowering young people to explore opportunities, learn from others, and establish relationships that can contribute to their personal and professional development.


As Bangladesh’s youth continue to navigate an evolving career landscape, platforms that encourage collaboration between aspiring professionals and established members of the corporate world remain increasingly relevant.

With Get-together 3.0, LinkedIn Community Bangladesh reaffirms its commitment to fostering professional connections, encouraging collaboration, and supporting the career aspirations of the next generation.
